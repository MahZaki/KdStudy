Chapter 07 · Financials

Hosting costs $19 a month until it needs to cost $209.

The whole application — API, background workers, and database — runs in a single Docker container, so infrastructure spend jumps in four discrete steps rather than climbing continuously with every new signup.

The four bands

UsersInfra / moNotes
0–75$19Single container, no proxy needed

151–350$150Transitional capacity tier
351+$209Current largest verified plan

By month 12, at 500 users and $8,745 in monthly revenue, infrastructure costs just $209 — roughly 2.4% of revenue. This is the single biggest reason margin expands rather than compresses with scale.
