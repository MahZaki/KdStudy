"Publish to KDP" button

One click from the finished listing opens Amazon's Create New Title page and injects the title, subtitle, author, description, keywords, and categories automatically.

Amazon stays the source of truth

The author still uploads the final files and hits save inside Amazon's own dashboard — this removes re-typing, not control or compliance.

A checklist, not a black box

The Launch screen tracks exactly what's done and what's left per project, so nothing gets published half-finished.
