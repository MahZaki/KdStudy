Chapter 03 · Product

Five stages. One session. Nothing re-typed.

Every research query becomes a tracked "project" that moves through the same five stages, automatically, so the author never starts over from a blank spreadsheet.

STAGE 1

Discover

Search a topic or generate niche ideas. The engine scores hundreds of live Amazon listings in real time.

→
STAGE 2

Validate

One market snapshot: is this niche penetrable? Green, yellow, or red — with the evidence behind it.

→
STAGE 3

Create

Turn the validated niche into a real book: interior, cover art, full-wrap cover PDF, KDP metadata.

→
STAGE 4

Launch

A checklist plus a browser extension that auto-fills Amazon's own publishing form.

→
STAGE 5

Published

Live on Amazon, generating royalties — and, from the software's point of view, a completed cycle.

Why the pipeline matters more than any single feature

Most keyword-research tools stop at Stage 1 and hand the author a spreadsheet. The gap between "here's a promising keyword" and "here's a book for sale" is where authors actually give up. Carrying one session all the way through is the product's real differentiator — and the reason a customer stays subscribed past month one.

What's threaded through every stage

A single session_id carries the research forward automatically — the app advances the stage on its own as work is completed, with a manual override always available. Nothing has to be copy-pasted between tools, because there's only one tool.
