Why the dip at 100 and 200 users

Margin isn't a straight line — it steps down slightly right after each infrastructure upgrade (see p.17) and climbs back as the new, larger server is filled with more paying users. At 100 and 200 users the business has just paid for capacity it hasn't fully grown into yet.

The takeaway for an investor

Profit per user stays remarkably flat — roughly $7.20–$8.90 no matter the scale. This is not a business that needs hypergrowth to become profitable; it's already profitable at 50 users, and stays that way at 10x that size.
