Live search not a database

Every query crawls Amazon's real, current search results and product pages — the app is never showing an investor last month's snapshot, and neither is it showing the author one.

Opportunity score one number

Sales rank, competition density, price, and rating are blended into a single 0–100 score per book, so a beginner can act on it without understanding Amazon's ranking algorithm.

Smart presets zero setup

One-tap filters — "low-hanging fruit," "hot right now," "new-entrant friendly" — surface the shortlist without the author writing a single filter query.

Niche generator idea engine

When an author has no starting keyword at all, a combinatorial engine crosses topics, book types, audiences, and goals to propose fresh angles no one has typed in yet.
