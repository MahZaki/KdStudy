Chapter 01 · Market

We don't sell to Amazon. We sell to
everyone trying to get noticed on it.

Think of KDP less like a bookstore and more like a search engine with a printing press attached. Our customer is anyone competing for rank inside it.

Global self-publishing market revenue. 2033 figure is a third-party projection at a reported 16.7% CAGR. Illustrative, not a forecast of our own revenue.

The numbers under the number

people have already published through KDP

31%

16.7%

None of that revenue is ours by default — we don't sell books. We sell the shovel: research, drafting, cover design, and publishing tooling to the ever-growing pool of people trying to strike a niche. As the pool of authors grows, so does the pool of people who need a faster, cheaper way to find where to dig.
