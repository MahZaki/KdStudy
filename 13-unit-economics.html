Two cost lenses, on purpose

"Worst case" assumes every credit a customer spends goes toward the single most expensive cover action possible. "Realistic" reflects actual blended usage across the credit system. The table above uses the realistic figure — even the worst-case floor still clears roughly 46–47% gross margin per user, shown below.
