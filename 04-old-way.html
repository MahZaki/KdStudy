Chapter 02 · Problem

Before a single word is written, a week is already gone.

This is a real, typical week for an unaided KDP author trying to find a profitable idea and get it market-ready — the exact workflow our product replaces.

Day 1–2

Keyword guessing

Manually typing ideas into Amazon's search bar, reading autocomplete suggestions, opening 30+ tabs of competing books one at a time.

~6 hrs
Day 2–3

Competitor stalking

Copying each competitor's price, review count, and Best Sellers Rank into a spreadsheet by hand, trying to eyeball whether the niche is "too crowded."

~5 hrs
Day 3–4

Gut-feel scoring

No consistent formula — just instinct on demand vs. competition, which is exactly how good ideas get killed and bad ones get greenlit.

~3 hrs
Day 5–6

Cover & listing

Hiring a freelance designer (days of back-and-forth) and hand-writing a title, description, and keyword list that may not follow Amazon's own listing rules.

~8 hrs
Day 7

Manual upload

Re-typing every field into KDP's publishing form by hand, praying nothing gets rejected on review.

~2 hrs
Total, before a single sentence of the book itself is written
~24 hrs

And the honest ending: most of that guesswork is still just guesswork. A spreadsheet of BSR numbers doesn't tell you why a niche is winnable, and a rejected KDP listing for a title-length rule nobody reads sends the author back to square one. The tooling on the market today is either a $50/month keyword-lookup tool that stops at step one, or nothing at all.
