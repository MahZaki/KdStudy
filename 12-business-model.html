Why flat allowances, not metered credits, for research

Search and competitor-analysis costs are pennies per call, so they're given as a fixed monthly allowance — simple to understand, simple to sell. Only the more expensive action, cover art generation, is metered by a credit system, which is where the real cost sensitivity lives.

The mix that drives the model

Every projection in this deck assumes the 55 / 35 / 10 tier mix shown above — a conservative, Starter-heavy split. If Pro and Studio adoption comes in even slightly higher, every margin number on the following pages moves up, not down.
