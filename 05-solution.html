Chapter 02 · Solution

One piece of software does the research,
writes the report, and hands you a book.

KDP Niche Scout replaces that 24-hour manual grind with a guided pipeline — software that scouts Amazon itself, scores what it finds, and can carry a validated idea all the way through to a publish-ready book.

Four things it actually does

01

Scouts

Crawls live Amazon search results and product pages to find real demand and real gaps — not a stale database.

02

Scores

Turns rank, competition, price, and reviews into one plain verdict: is this niche worth the author's time or not.

03

Creates

Drafts the interior, generates cover art, and writes a KDP-compliant listing with an AI model doing the heavy lifting.

04

Launches

A companion browser extension fills Amazon's own publishing form automatically — no re-typing.
