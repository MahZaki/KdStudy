Chapter 04 · Defensibility

The boring part is the moat: staying online when Amazon fights back.

Amazon actively tries to block automated readers of its own pages. Every keyword-research tool in this category lives or dies on how well it survives that — and this one is built with a three-tier fallback system specifically to survive it.

LEVEL 0

Fast & light

Plain, high-speed requests with a spoofed browser fingerprint. Handles the large majority of traffic cheaply.

LEVEL 0 (RETRY)

New fingerprint

If blocked, retries automatically with a different signature before escalating further.

LEVEL 1

Stealth browser

A real, disguised headless browser for pages that specifically detect bots.

LEVEL 2

Full browser

Last resort: a complete browser running real JavaScript, for the pages nothing else can reach.

Runs on real, current data

Because every session is a live crawl, not a stale licensed dataset, results reflect the shelf exactly as it looks right now — the thing a paid, static database can't promise.

Five markets already live

US, Germany, France, Italy, and Spain are each mapped with their own verified search filters — an install base most single-market competitors haven't bothered to build.

Never leaves a job stuck

Every background job — scrape, AI call, or render — runs under a deadline and reconciles itself on restart, so a crashed worker never leaves a paying customer staring at a spinner.

None of this is exotic technology. It's disciplined engineering, accumulated over real production incidents — which is exactly why it's slow and expensive for a competitor to copy in a weekend, and exactly why it's still standing.
