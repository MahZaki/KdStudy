Chapter 01 · Primer

Amazon will publish anyone's book. For free. Tonight.

That's the whole idea of KDP — Kindle Direct Publishing. No agent, no publisher, no gatekeeper. Upload a file, and by tomorrow it's for sale next to every other book on Amazon.

Traditional publishing

Write a book → find an agent → find a publisher → wait 12–24 months → publisher prints and distributes it → you get ~10–15% of each sale.

vs.

KDP self-publishing

Write a book → upload it to Amazon yourself → it's live within 72 hours → you keep 35–70% of each sale, paid monthly, forever.

Amazon built the entire storefront, the payment system, the printing press (books are printed on demand, one at a time, when someone actually buys), and the shelf space. Anyone can walk in. Over a million people already have. The catch is exactly the catch you'd expect: if anyone can publish anything, most of the shelf is noise. Finding a corner of that shelf where real buyers are searching, competition is thin, and a book can be produced quickly enough to matter — that's the entire skill of doing this well. It's also, not coincidentally, the entire product this deck is about.

Five words your model will want translated

KDP
Kindle Direct Publishing — Amazon's self-service publishing platform, for e-books and print-on-demand paperbacks alike.
Niche
A specific, searchable topic + book-type combination — e.g. "left-handed calligraphy workbook for teens," not "workbooks."
BSR
Best Sellers Rank — Amazon's own live scoreboard of how well a book is selling relative to every other book, updated hourly.
Royalty
The author's cut of each sale — up to 70% on e-books, 40–60% on print, paid out monthly by Amazon directly.
